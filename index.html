PET RECOVERY TIPS

What to do when a pet goes missing

Staying organized and acting quickly can make a big difference.

01

Search nearby first

Check your home, street, nearby parks, familiar walking routes, and places where your pet may hide.

02

Share a clear photo

Use recent photographs that show unique markings, collars, colors, and facial features.

03

Tell your community

Ask trusted friends and neighbors to watch for the pet and share the report.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good to know

What information should I include? +

Include a clear photo, pet type, color, distinctive markings, location, date last seen, and a reliable way to contact you.

What should I do if I find a pet? +

Keep the pet somewhere safe if you can, check nearby reports, and create a Found report with a clear description and photograph.

Should I share my exact home address? +

Avoid publishing sensitive personal information. A neighborhood, nearby landmark, or general area is usually enough for an initial report.

How can I help if I haven't found a pet? +

Sharing reports with people in your neighborhood can dramatically increase the number of people who see them.
